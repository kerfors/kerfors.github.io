Kerstin Forsberg

Information architect, working with clinical data standards, linked data and AI-readiness. Based in the Göteborg area, Sweden.

I am doing this semi-retired, after 25 years in clinical trial data, on problems that matter. Problems that now can be addressed by using AI to apply sound information architecture principles on existing models and vocabulary standards - and on the data itself, when it actually uses them.

Work

  • CDISC for AI Explorative work on the behaviour of the CDISC standards, with machine-actionable reference files built from SDTM CT, NCIt and COSMoS.
  • usdm-rdf The CDISC Unified Study Definitions Model (USDM) v4 rendered as RDF/OWL, generated mechanically from the published model. Draft.
  • cosmos-rdf CDISC COSMoS (Biomedical Concepts and SDTM Dataset Specializations) rendered as RDF/OWL, as published. Early, exploratory work.
  • SoA2USDM Collections Machine-actionable extractions of Schedules of Activities from published clinical trial protocols.
